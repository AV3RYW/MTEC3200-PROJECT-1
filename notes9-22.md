Two out of the three people I interviewed had the problem of repeatedly snoozing their alarms and struggling to get prepared for their day on time. What they do afterward to make up for lost time is that they miss things they have to do while they get ready, or just intentionally not do something to try to get that time back. They say that happens because they know they need the extra time in the morning to get ready and get their day started, but they will see what time it is and remember what time their job or class starts, and then go right back to sleep. Another reason why this happens to them is that they will go to bed late and will want to go back to bed.

The app is designed to help people who have a hard time going to sleep and waking up using positive and negative feedback. What it will do is it will have notifications based on whether they woke up on time or overslept; if you overslept, it will send you a notification and some stats on what you could have done with the time you slept, and if you woke up on time, it will give some positive feedback and words of encouragement.
