This tool will help them make healthier routine habits in the morning so they don't waste their mornings oversleeping 

It will have them put in their desired time they want to wake up and will see if they are waking up on time; if they aren't, the app will then show them everything they could have done with their time if they didn’t oversleep

Things I'm tempted to add is a streak system so they can share with their friends how many days they woke up on time, and something to show off like Wordle or Duolingo 

Things that are out of scope is keeping track of bedtime because it will become confusing, and the average 7 hours of sleep won't be taken  into consideration
